# Mini-Launch — Your First Signal

The quick win of the Define phase. Within **48 hours** of finishing your Pricing Strategy, put your offer — in your persona's own words — in front of real potential customers, and get at least one response back.

The win bar is deliberately low: **one person replying counts.** The point is not statistical proof; it's breaking the seal on building in private. The first time a real human responds to your product, it stops being a document and starts being a business. A reply is *contact*, not *validation* — validation happens by climbing the **signal ladder** across the whole program: this launch gets a reply, the Design relaunch gets a "can I try it?", the beta invite gets an activated user, the public launch gets a payment. This worksheet's results become the first entry in `docs/LAUNCHES.md` — the launch log every later relaunch builds on.

Fill in each section below. Keep answers short — this worksheet is filled in twice: before you ship (sections 1–2) and after (sections 3–6).

## 1. Your Audience Access

*Where can you reach people who match your persona, starting today?*

Pick the **warmest channel you actually have** — not the one you wish you had.

| Route | Pick when | The ask | The win |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The DM Ask** | You have clients, past customers, or a network that matches the persona | A personal message to 5–10 named people | One reply |
| **The Community Post** | No audience, but your persona congregates somewhere named (subreddit, Discord, Slack, forum) | "I'm building X for Y because Z — does this resonate? What am I missing?" | One substantive comment |
| **The Build-in-Public Post** | You're active on X / LinkedIn / TikTok — or you're mid-build and have screenshots to show | "Here's what I'm building and why" + a screenshot of what exists so far | One reply or DM |
| **The One-Pager** *(optional upgrade)* | Any of the above, plus an hour to spare | A one-page site with an email box, shared via one of the routes above | One signup from a stranger |

> Good: a named channel you can use tomorrow morning — a list of 8 real people, "r/freelance", your own LinkedIn feed
> Bad: "I'll post everywhere", a community you've never posted in and haven't checked the rules of, waiting to build an audience first

## 4. Proof — Share the Live Post

*A screenshot of the LIVE post or sent DM, shared in your cohort community — or, if you're working solo, with an accountability partner or your public build-in-public feed.*

The thing you share with the group is the **sent artifact** — the live post with its timestamp, the DM in your sent folder — not a draft for feedback. Real proof, posted the day you ship. This keeps the bar honest: shipped means shipped, and the people you share with see it.

> Good: a screenshot of the live post/DM with the timestamp visible, posted in the community the same day
> Bad: a draft "for feedback before I send it", a description of what you're planning to post

## 5. The Signal Log

*Every response, logged — including silence.*

Log each response with: who, what they said, **warm or cold** (warm = they know you; cold = stranger), and the date. Friend replies count — but label them, because "your only reply was your co-worker" is a different data point from "a stranger asked for the price."

**The signal ladder** — one reply is the win; each rung up is worth more. The same ladder runs through the whole program — each phase's launch targets the next rung:

`reply → conversation → signup → activated user → payment`

> Good: every response logged, warm/cold labelled, ladder rung noted — including "0 replies after 10 DMs"
> Bad: counting only the praise, not logging silence, treating one enthusiastic friend as market proof

## 6. What the Signal Means

*Your read, and your next move.*

- **One or more replies** — win. You have contact with the market. Follow every thread: a real conversation is rung two, and "can I try it?" is a person to put your Magic Moment in front of later.
- **A conversation or better** — pull their exact words into `2-Customer-Persona.md` (pain language), and add the person to the believers list in `docs/LAUNCHES.md`. Your believers are the first invite at every later launch: the Design preview, the beta, the paid launch.
- **Zero replies after the full send** — also data. Rewrite the message once (different pain hook, add or remove the price) and send through your next-warmest route. Still zero? Log it in `docs/LAUNCHES.md` and read `BONUS-Pivot-Framework.md` — you've learned something in week two that most founders learn in month six.

> Good: a one-sentence honest read plus a dated next action — "2 warm replies, 1 cold; booking a call with the cold one Friday"
> Bad: "people seemed to like it", reading one reply as validation, reading zero replies as failure instead of data
