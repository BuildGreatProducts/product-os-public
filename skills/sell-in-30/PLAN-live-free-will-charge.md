*The product is live and free. No price on the site, no payments integration. The member will charge within the thirty days. The bar is a payment.*

## Notes for the composer

- **Day 1 is two skills (three when `define-from-code` has to run first), and it fits** because the offer review is a critique of what exists (not the builder's interview) and the pricing session takes about thirty minutes on its fast path (a single outcome, one billing unit, one or two plans — the case for most products on this path). If `docs/DEFINE.md` is missing, `define-from-code` reads the live product first; the whole day is still one sitting.
- **The price goes on the page on Day 2, before the checkout exists.** A page that names the price and a "get started" that leads to a waitlist for one day is fine; a warm ask on Day 4 to a page with no price is not.
- **Day 3's test purchase is real money, refunded.** Test-mode keys are not the proof; the member has to see a live receipt.
- **Week one's warm ask is the most likely source of the first payment.** Don't let the strategy work on Days 5–6 delay Day 4.
