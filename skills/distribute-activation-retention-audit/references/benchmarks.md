# Calibration benchmarks

Directional figures for calibrating severity and for the report's *Sources & calibration* section. Calibrate every one to the product's retention model before scoring.

## Benchmarks — last reviewed October 2026 (re-verify with web search before quoting)

- **Activation → conversion.** Users who reach the aha in their first session (or within 48h) are markedly more likely to convert to paid — a figure of ~3.4× has been used here. *Source: not traced to a primary study; vendor blogs commonly repeat a 3–5× retention lift for users who reach the aha early (correlational). Quote as directional only.* A large share of signups never reach first value when a setup wall precedes it.
- **Onboarding tour length.** 3-step tours complete at ~72%, 7-step at ~16%. *Source: Chameleon Benchmark Report (as cited by Userpilot, "Product tours"); other Chameleon editions report ~66% for 3-step tours.*
- **Day 1 retention.** Baseline ~26%, optimized 40%+. *Source: not recorded — verify before quoting.*
- **Retention curves.** Most consumer apps lose the majority of users in the first week; the curve should *flatten* (a stable returning core), not trend to zero. B2B SaaS healthy logo retention is high (90%+ annual); consumer varies widely by category. *Source: general industry pattern — verify a category-specific figure before quoting.*
- **Re-engagement.** Lifecycle email and (where it fits) push meaningfully lift retention vs. transactional-only; a welcome series is the highest-leverage first send. *Source: practitioner consensus, no single figure.*
- **Passive churn.** Involuntary/failed-payment churn is often ~20–40% of total subscription churn, and the cheapest to recover with smart retries + dunning emails. *Source: a commonly repeated industry range (subscription-billing vendor glossaries); no primary study traced. PYMNTS has reported failed payments behind up to ~50% of subscription churn.*
