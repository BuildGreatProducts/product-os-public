## Notes for the composer

- **Existing users are the warm network.** Day 4's asks go to the people already using the product, by name, before anyone else. "You've been using X for a month; I'm putting a price on it this week; here's the founding price for you" is the whole message.
- **The founding offer is drafted from the member's own anchors** (`3-Pricing-Strategy.md`'s price anchors and the Product Offer's guarantee), each time. No house pattern. It is an experiment with a Pass = line, not a permanent price change.
- **Activation matters here too.** If the evidence says users sign up and don't come back, `studio-distribute-activation-retention-audit` belongs high in the up-next queue; asking someone to pay for a product they stopped using is a persona or product signal the pivot review will read.
