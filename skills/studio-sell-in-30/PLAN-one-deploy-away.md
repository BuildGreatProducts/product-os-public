# Sell in 30 — week one: Not yet live, one deploy away

*The app runs locally, deploy config or `docs/DEPLOY.md` is in progress, and the member can be live within the first few days. Anyone further from live than that belongs in `studio-ship-in-7`. The bar is a payment (or an activated user, if the product stays free this month).*

**Honest note at enrol:** this is the tightest week one in the challenge. Two sessions of deploy push the offer review, messaging, checkout, warm asks, strategy and experiments into five. Say so, and recommend more hours for this week than for the rest.

## Notes for the composer

- **The security audit is not optional because the week is tight.** If no `docs/SECURITY-AUDIT.md` exists, it runs on Day 1 before the deploy guide; Critical findings are fixed before anything is reachable. Ship in 7's quality-gate rule applies here too.
- **If the deploy takes a third session**, it takes it: Day 3 becomes deploy, and Days 3–4 as written merge into Day 4 (messaging drafted from the review, checkout live, warm asks in one long session). The read stays on Day 7.
- **If the deploy can't be done in three sessions**, the honest move is to stop, log it, and route to `studio-ship-in-7`. The member restarts Sell in 30 the week the app is live, with the "after Ship in 7" plan.

## Compression (a missed or short session)

1. Merge Days 5–6.
2. Merge Day 3's messaging into Day 2's review: draft and ship in one session.
3. Never move the checkout after the warm asks. Never move the read.
