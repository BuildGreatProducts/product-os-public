## 1.11.0 — September 2026

**The Pricing Strategy now owns the model, not just the number.** "$29" is not a price until it's "$29 per workspace per month, one plan, 14-day trial" — but the week-one pricing session deliberately stopped at the number and parked the business model and billing unit in the optional deep dive, so members reached the Mini-Launch, the PRD's payment section, and the GTM payback check with a number that had no unit behind it. Now one 30–45 minute session decides how the product earns, what the price multiplies by, and the number, with sixteen business models and twenty-four pricing mechanics behind it.

- **Every skill drops the `studio-` prefix** — `studio-define-pricing` is now `define-pricing`, `studio-launch` is `mini-launch`, `studio-setup` is `setup`, `studio-ship-in-7` / `studio-sell-in-30` are `ship-in-7` / `sell-in-30`; the three build loops were already unprefixed. Folder names, frontmatter names, and every cross-reference in the checklists, templates, guides, challenge plans, and setup files are renamed together. `setup` reads the old prefixed names in a pre-1.11.0 `docs/PLAN.md` as aliases, so coached plans keep working unchanged.
- **`define/3-Pricing-Strategy.md` reworked** — four sections become six: Who Pays (now with the shape of the value), **How the Business Earns** (pick one of sixteen models, each linked to its reference section, with the rejected alternative written down), **Pricing Model** (billing unit, plans, entry, cadence, extra use, said as one sentence), Price Anchors, Launch Price (one number triangulated from a value ceiling, a heavy-use cost floor, and the anchors, with a starting-bands table and a must-be-positive sanity line), and Your Price Line.
- **`define-pricing` reworked** — routes between a ~30 minute fast path (one continuous or bounded outcome, one unit, one or two plans) and a full path for usage/AI-heavy, marketplace, ads, B2B, services, open-core, and outcome-based products; reads only the reference sections that match the hypothesis; builds the cost floor from a cheat sheet instead of skipping it. Twelve named failure patterns, including The Layer Blur, The Invisible Unit, The Unlimited Promise, The Ten-Percent Rule, and The Launch-Cash Mirage.
- **New reference `define/BONUS-Business-Models.md`** — the sixteen ways an app earns (subscription through lifetime offers), each with how it works, best-fit apps, what tends to work and fail, a worked economics example, what to measure, distribution implications, a first experiment, and sources — opened by a value-frequency decision table.
- **New reference `define/BONUS-Pricing-Models.md`** — twenty-four billing units, packaging shapes, quantity calculations, payment cadences, and entry routes, plus three chapters on setting the price (value, cost, alternatives, and a delivery-cost cheat sheet), anchoring and plan presentation, and discounts, retention, and guarantees — opened by the keep-the-layers-separate table and three worked combinations.
- **`BONUS-Business-Strategy-Deep-Dive.md` slimmed to three sections** — Revenue Model and Pricing Ladder move into the Pricing Strategy; Cost & Margin gains a low / expected / heavy-use contribution table; Unfair Advantage is unchanged; the North Star table is keyed by business model and gains a usage-based row. `define-business-strategy` now reads the Pricing Strategy as given and routes any price the economics break back to `define-pricing`.
- Downstream readers updated: `define-product`'s Business model section, `develop-prd-roadmap` and `PRD-GENERATION.md` (plans and limits now come from the Pricing Strategy), `distribute-gtm-strategy`, `define-from-code` (infers model and unit from Stripe products or the listing), `mini-launch`, the Define checklist, the Pivot Framework's links, and the intro of `BONUS-Real-Business-Strategy-Examples.md` (which both pricing skills now read).

## 1.10.0 — September 2026

**Two challenges for the community: Ship in 7 and Sell in 30.** Members installing the public copy had one route — the linear checklists — and no accountability beyond their own calendar. The two new cross-phase skills give them a composed plan with a proof every session, a Skool post that keeps the bar honest, and a close that hands them to the Product Studio with their situation already written down.

- **New skill `studio-ship-in-7`** — seven sessions to the app live at a real URL. Reads the repo, shows what exists, asks which starting point fits (idea only, AI-generated app, local prototype, prompt-to-app platform), and composes a session-by-session plan from the existing skills: one block, one skill, one proof. The bar is a live URL and a smoke test passed as a real customer; the quality gate (`studio-develop-security-audit`) is never skipped and go-live never moves past Day 7. A daily check-in confirms the last session's proof before naming today's block, applies compression rules on a miss, and drafts a Skool post in the member's own voice. The close writes the Ship Report into `docs/SHIP-IN-7.md` — shaped to be brought to a Product Studio call — and recommends the programme. Ships with `SHIP-IN-7-TEMPLATE.md` and four worked `PLAN-*.md` files.
- **New skill `studio-sell-in-30`** — thirty sessions to the first paying customer (or the first activated user, for a product staying free that month). Week one: `studio-define-offer-review` always, every acquisition surface brought in line with the reviewed offer and shipped live, go-live if the member is one deploy away, checkout live with a real test purchase, warm-network asks through `studio-launch`, then `studio-distribute-gtm-strategy` and `studio-distribute-growth-experiments` briefed with the bar and the clock. Weeks two to four run the top experiment from the member's own backlog, one a week: every session that reaches people ends with a six-field feedback (step, reach, warm/cold, response and rung, verbatim, surprise) written to an **Experiment Log**, and every seventh session is a weekly read — the signal read, **continue or kill** against the experiment's Pass = line, and a **pivot review** that diagnoses distribution, execution and patience before suggesting at most one of persona, product or pricing, written as a candidate experiment. Weekly Skool posts; the close writes the Sell Report as the pre-call brief. Ships with `SELL-IN-30-TEMPLATE.md` and six week-one `PLAN-*.md` files.
- **Both challenges** run after `studio-setup` and run it first if the repo isn't wired; count sessions rather than calendar days (consecutive recommended; a gap is not a miss, a session without proof is); scale one plan to the member's hours rather than offering tiers; never post, message, or change a live price on the member's behalf; and end with the same recommendation — book a call at buildgreatproducts.com/product-studio and bring the report. Only one challenge is open at a time. The daily-loop mechanics are deliberately duplicated in both `SKILL.md` files: a change to one loop is a change to both.
- **Wiring:** `setup/CLAUDE.md` + `setup/AGENTS.md` gain the rule that an open challenge file starts every session with its check-in, and the challenge logs join the canonical docs list (existing repos pick this up on their next `studio-setup` re-run). `studio-setup`'s no-plan route offers a challenge before the linear checklists; `studio-launch` reads the open challenge for its send deadline; `studio-develop-golive` fits its estimate to the sessions left; `studio-distribute-gtm-strategy` and `studio-distribute-growth-experiments` take the bar and the clock from `docs/SELL-IN-30.md`. All four checklists gain a one-line pointer to the open challenge. README, START-HERE and AGENTS.md name four cross-phase skills.
- Skill count 38 → 40 across the README and all plugin manifests; codex `defaultPrompt` gains "Start ship in 7 (or sell in 30)".

## 1.9.0 — August 2026

**The product gets a copy system the way it already gets a design system.** Members build their UIs with coding agents, and an LLM's default register is marketing English — verbose, abstract, apologetic, exclamatory. Nothing owned the in-product copy: buttons, labels, errors, empty states, confirmations, toasts, notifications, terminology. Now `docs/COPY.md` does — DESIGN.md is how the product looks; COPY.md is how it speaks.

- **New skill `studio-design-ux-writing`** — Design checklist Step 2, right after the Product Identity. Translates the Identity's Tone of Voice into an operational copy system: a voice chart (every cell a followable rule), a terminology lexicon (one name per thing), per-surface rules with product-specific good/bad pairs, the product's 3–5 real error messages fully written, a banned-word list, and a per-screen review rubric. Routes by arrival state — greenfield builds the guide from the design docs; a repo with real UI strings additionally gets an audit pass that classifies offenders by anti-pattern and appends an executable fix list; a repo without ProductOS runs a standalone fallback (codebase + three tone questions).
- **New reference `design/BONUS-UX-Writing-Best-Practice.md`** — the source of truth the skill cites by tactic number: a meta-rule (clear beats concise beats consistent beats voice beats wit), 12 principles, a which-kind-of-string decision tree, 20 tactics across 8 surface stages (including the Polaris verb dictionary and the three-part error anatomy), 3 worked examples, and 12 named anti-patterns (The Marketing Bleed, The Are-You-Sure Dialog, The Synonym Shuffle, …) — distilled from Apple HIG, Material, Microsoft, Polaris, Mailchimp, Atlassian, GOV.UK, NN/g, Podmajersky, and Yifrah.
- **`setup/CLAUDE.md` + `setup/AGENTS.md` gain §7 Copy Fidelity** — "the copy guide is law": coding agents read `docs/COPY.md` before touching any user-facing string, write from the lexicon, and run the rubric on every screen they change. Existing member repos pick this up on their next `studio-setup` re-run (same as §6 in 1.5.0). `COPY.md` joins the canonical docs list.
- Design checklist renumbered: UX Writing Guide is Step 2; Design System, Prompts, Magic Moment, Onboarding, Acquisition, and Relaunch shift to 3–8. The three skills that name Steps 2–3 (`identity-creator`, `design-system`, `prompt-generator`) and the Product Identity template are renumbered to match; the identity-creator's closing now routes to the UX writing session first.
- Downstream skills read the guide when present: the prompt generator embeds the lexicon so AI-generated screens are copy-correct; onboarding flow, landing page, and app listing conform their copy to it; `design-better`, `design-review`, `cro-audit`, and `prd-roadmap` check or inherit it.
- Skill count 37 → 38 across the README and all plugin manifests.

## 1.7.0 — August 2026

**Escaping the prompt-to-app platforms.** Members arriving from Lovable, Bolt, v0, or Base44 needed a safe path into their own repo, stack, and coding agent — and members refactoring an existing app no longer need to run the full PRD interview first.

- **New skill `studio-develop-migrate`** — moves an app off a prompt-to-app platform completely, safely, and once. Inventories everything the platform manages (hosting, database, auth, secrets, storage, edge functions, cron jobs — and the easy-to-miss inbound webhooks like Stripe endpoints pointing at platform URLs), recommends the target stack on a keep-what-you-own rule, researches the platform's current export mechanics live (they change fast — Lovable only shipped official backend Export/Pause/Remove controls in mid-2026), then writes `docs/MIGRATION.md`: a phased checkbox plan ending in a verification gate — real login with a pre-migration password, test payment, storage URLs — all green before the old platform is ever paused. ## 1.5.0 — July 2026

**The build phase is now Develop, and it gained its quality gates.** The research on AI-built founder apps is blunt — most AI-generated code works and is insecure, and the incidents that killed real apps came from a short list: committed secrets, databases without row-level security, unprotected API routes, missing ownership checks, and secret keys shipped to the browser.

- **Deploy renamed to Develop** across the system: `develop/` folder, `DEVELOP-CHECKLIST.md`, all nine skills `studio-deploy-*` → `studio-develop-*` (precedent: 1.0.0's `studio-build-*` → `studio-deploy-*`), phase prose everywhere (Define → Design → **Develop** → Distribute), and the coach catalog's "Develop route". `docs/DEPLOY.md` keeps its name — it's genuinely the deployment guide. Develop-phase skill H1s normalised to `# Develop: …`.
- **New skill `studio-develop-code-review`** — the deliberate pre-commit pass over uncommitted changes (including new untracked files, where stray secrets land). States the change's intent, reviews across five lenses, verifies every finding against real source (`file:line`, never inference), reports must-fix / consider / **pre-existing** (landmines surfaced without blaming today's diff), and always ends with a verdict: ready to commit or not. Re-reviews report must-fix only — the loop converges. Complements, not duplicates, the build loops' in-flight `/review`.
- **New skill `studio-develop-security-audit`** — full audit of the codebase (or uncommitted changes), ordered by what actually burns founder apps: secrets, RLS/database rules, unprotected routes, IDOR, browser-reachable secret keys, then injection/webhooks/CORS/dependencies/rate-limiting, then headers/CSRF/uploads. Stack-aware (managed auth ⇒ whole categories N/A), verifies every finding to a concrete exploit path, and knows the key-identity rules (anon key public by design; `service_role`/`sk_live_` client-reachable = Critical; RLS-off is the real finding). Produces **`docs/SECURITY-AUDIT.md`**: verdict first, "Do this right now" for leaked credentials (rotate before fixing), a severity-ordered checkbox Fix plan with falsifiable Verify lines a coding agent can execute while the member keeps building, and separate Human-only actions. Never auto-fixes.
- Develop checklist regains its arc with the gates in place: Step 6 code review before commit, Step 9 security audit before go-live (steps renumbered, now 10). The build loops accept the audit's Fix plan as an executable plan file.
- **`setup/CLAUDE.md` + `setup/AGENTS.md` gained "6. Secure by Default"** — the preventive layer wired into every app repo root: no committed secrets, server-side ownership checks, rules on every table from day one, parameterized queries, verified webhooks, no `service_role`/`sk_live_` in the client.
- Skill count 33 → 35.

## 1.1.0 — July 2026

The program now front-loads a quick win and threads it through all four phases as **the launch ladder**: `reply → conversation → signup → activated user → payment`, tracked in `docs/LAUNCHES.md`.

- **Define slimmed to 5 steps:** Product Offer → Customer Persona → **Pricing Strategy** (new) → **Mini-Launch** (new, ★ the quick win) → PRODUCT.md. The whole phase fits in the first two weeks.
- New template `define/3-Pricing-Strategy.md` + skill `studio-define-pricing` (20–30 min): who pays, one launch price said out loud, real anchors, and the price line the mini-launch carries.
- New template `define/4-Mini-Launch.md` + cross-phase skill **`studio-launch`**: routes by audience access (DM ask / community post / build-in-public post / optional one-pager), drafts the message in the persona's language, timeboxes shipping to 48 hours, and logs every response. **The win is one reply.** Proof is a screenshot of the **live** post or sent DM shared with the cohort — never a draft.
