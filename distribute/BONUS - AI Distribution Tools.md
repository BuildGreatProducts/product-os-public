# AI Tools for Distribution & Go-To-Market

A curated stack for getting your product in front of people — across organic content, SEO, AI search, outreach, paid ads, influencer, and email.

**How this list is organised.** Each section leads with **Y Combinator–backed startups** (the newest wave, batch noted), then the **established, highly-rated tools** most builders already trust. You don't need all of these. Pick one per channel that matches where your customers actually are, and go deep before adding a second.

> Batch codes: W = Winter, S = Summer, F = Fall. So "YC S23" = Summer 2023.

---

## 1. Organic social & short-form video

The highest-leverage channel for most early founders — it's free, it compounds, and people buy from people. Best place to start if you're pre-revenue.

**YC-backed**
- **Typa** *(YC S24)* — Learns your tone and turns your thinking into posts for X, LinkedIn, and newsletters. Has an AI ghostwriter ("Puff") you brief over WhatsApp/phone. Good for founder-led content.
- **Bluma** *(YC F25)* — AI short-form video engine. Clones the structure of competitors' viral videos and produces variations at scale.
- **Kite** *(YC S23)* — Turns a plain screen recording into a polished, Apple-style demo video with auto-zoom and 3D device mockups. Used by hundreds of YC companies for launch clips and feature demos. **Great fit for app builders.**
- **Wondercraft** *(YC S22)* — Create video and audio (incl. podcasts) through natural conversation.
- **Syncly** *(YC W23)* — AI social listening across TikTok/IG/YouTube — reads video and on-screen context, not just captions.
- **Imagine AI** *(YC F25)* — Coordinated LinkedIn content for a founding team (CEO + sales + marketing posting aligned narratives).

**Established & highly-rated**
- **OpusClip** — The category leader for turning long videos (podcasts, streams, talking-head) into short clips with a "virality score." Has a free tier. Start here for repurposing.
- **Submagic** — Animated captions, B-roll, and effects that make shorts pop. Often paired with OpusClip as the polish layer.
- **Buffer** — Clean, affordable scheduling across all platforms. The default for solo creators.
- **Taplio** — The household name for LinkedIn growth (swipe file of viral posts + scheduler + AI). Enter above the bottom tier to get the AI features.
- **Hypefury** — X (Twitter)-first scheduling and growth.
- **Vizard / Descript** — Strong alternatives for clipping and transcript-based editing.

## 2. SEO & content marketing

Slow to start, but durable traffic that doesn't depend on an algorithm's mood.

**YC-backed**
- **Rankai** *(YC S23)* — Autonomous SEO/GEO agent. Finds the queries your customers search, builds optimised pages, then tracks and rewrites them to improve. Aimed at SMBs/founders who'd otherwise hire an agency.
- **Gauge** *(YC S24)* — Unifies GA4, Search Console, keywords, and ads into one agent that does the work of a marketing team and helps you show up in AI answers.

**Established & highly-rated**
- **Surfer SEO** — The gold standard for on-page optimisation. Real-time content scoring, integrates with Google Docs/WordPress.
- **Frase** — Affordable research-to-publish workflow; great for solo creators and small teams (~$45/mo).
- **Writesonic** — Budget all-in-one writer with built-in SEO *and* AI-search (GEO) tracking. Strong value (~$16–49/mo).
- **Clearscope** — Premium content optimisation for editorial teams (A–F grading).
- **Jasper** — Marketing-team AI writer with strong brand-voice training; integrates with Surfer.
- **Scalenut** — All-in-one keyword research + drafting + optimisation.

## 3. GEO / AEO — getting found *inside* AI answers

The newest channel, and arguably the most important one to plant a flag in now. When buyers ask ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini for recommendations, you want to be named. The data you collect today becomes your baseline.

**YC-backed**
- **AthenaHQ** *(YC W25)* — GEO/AEO platform built by ex-Google Search and DeepMind engineers. Tracks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, and more; ties visibility to GA4/Search Console. (Customers incl. Wix, SoFi.)
- **The Prompting Company** *(YC S25)* — Finds the exact questions your users ask AI, tells you what/where to post, and routes AI traffic to optimised pages. Offers a free AI-visibility report.
- **Bear** *(YC F25)* — Captures and converts the high-intent visitors arriving from ChatGPT, Perplexity, and other AI agents.
- **sitefire** *(YC W26)* — An AI-native agency that creates the content and on/off-page actions that get your product discovered and used by AI agents.
- **Unusual** *(YC F24)* — Diagnoses *why* AI models misperceive your brand and builds content to shift it. More enterprise.

**Established & highly-rated**
- **Otterly.ai** — The most accessible entry point (~$29/mo). Tracks brand mentions/citations across the major AI engines. Best for startups and lean teams. (Gartner Cool Vendor 2025.)
- **Peec AI** — Mid-market favourite (~€85/mo). Maps the citation network — which third-party sites feed your mentions — so you know where to do PR. Unlimited seats.
- **Profound** — The market leader (raised ~$96M, ~$1B valuation; G2's AEO category leader). Deep "Prompt Volumes" data and crawler analytics. Enterprise-grade.
- **HubSpot AI Search Grader** — Free one-time snapshot of how AI engines see your brand. Good for a first pulse check before paying for anything.

## 4. Outreach & lead generation

For B2B / higher-ticket products. Modern tools split into **data/signals** (who to contact and when) and **sending** (getting emails to the inbox). Most good setups combine one of each.

**YC-backed**
- **Gojiberry AI** *(YC Spring '26)* — An "AI GTM team": finds buyers showing intent, enriches, reaches out, handles replies, books meetings. ~$2.5M ARR in 10 months; learns from what converts.
- **AiSDR** *(YC S23)* — Automates prospecting end-to-end — writes and sends emails, then replies and books meetings.
- **Topo** *(YC W24)* — AI outbound SDR agent trained on your product and ICP; surfaces buying signals. EU-based, good for European outreach.
- **Coldreach** *(YC W23)* — Finds *ready-to-buy* leads by monitoring public signals custom to your product (e.g. who just hired a role you sell into).
- **Leadbay** *(YC F25)* — Qualifies hard-to-find leads that databases miss; customers say it beats stitching together Clay + an agency.
- **Origami** *(YC F24)* — Prompt to find and enrich leads across 100+ data sources, then launch a sequence. Free to start.
- **Persana AI** *(YC W23)* — Agentic GTM layer that researches, enriches, and engages across 100+ data sources.
- **Popsy** *(YC S22)* — Finds leads on Reddit and sends hyper-personalised DMs. Niche but effective for community-led products.

**Established & highly-rated**
- **Apollo.io** — The most popular all-in-one: 270M+ contact database + sequencing, with a usable free plan. Best single starting point.
- **Clay** — The enrichment/personalisation powerhouse that feeds every other tool. Steeper learning curve, huge payoff.
- **Instantly** — High-volume cold email with best-in-class deliverability and a large warm-up network.
- **Smartlead** — High-volume sending with unlimited mailboxes; agency-grade rotation and white-label.
- **Lemlist** — Best for *personalised, multichannel* (email + LinkedIn + calls). Ideal for solo founders doing high-touch outreach.
- **Woodpecker** — Deliverability-first with predictable pricing for small teams.

## A starter stack (if you only pick a few)

For a non-technical founder distributing a new product, this covers the most ground for the least money:

1. **Content engine:** OpusClip + Submagic (video) and/or Typa (written) → **Buffer** to schedule
2. **Get found in AI search:** **Otterly.ai** (start) + free **HubSpot AI Search Grader** audit
3. **SEO foundation:** **Frase** or **Writesonic**
4. **Outreach (if B2B):** **Apollo.io** for data + **Lemlist** or **Instantly** to send
5. **Ads (when you have budget):** **AdCreative.ai** for static, **Creatify** or **Arcads** for video
6. **Email/retention:** **Beehiiv** (newsletter) or **Loops** (product email)

**Rule of thumb:** master one channel before adding another. Tools amplify a strategy — they don't replace one.
