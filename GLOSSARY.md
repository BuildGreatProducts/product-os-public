# Glossary

Plain-English meanings for the words ProductOS and your coding agent use. Agents: when one of these comes up for the first time in a conversation, give the member the short meaning in passing — a few words, not a lecture.

## Your product

- **Offer** — one sentence on who the product is for, the problem it solves, and what it promises. Everything else is built on it.
- **Shape** — the form the product takes: a web app, a mobile app, an agent skill, a productized service, and so on. It decides which steps you run and what "live" means for you.
- **Persona** — a detailed picture of your ideal customer: what they do, what bothers them, and what they'll pay for.
- **Magic moment** — the instant a new customer first sees the value and decides to keep using the product.
- **Onboarding** — a new customer's first few minutes, designed to get them to the magic moment fast.
- **Acquisition surface** — the place a stranger decides to try you: a landing page, an App Store listing, a marketplace listing.
- **Live** — real customers can find and use the product. Your shape file says exactly what counts.
- **First sale** — the first time someone pays. Your shape file says exactly what counts.

## Your programme

- **Phase** — one of the four stages: Define (what you're building and for whom), Design (how it looks, sounds, and wins customers), Develop (building it and going live), Distribute (finding customers and growing).
- **Step** — one piece of work inside a phase, run by one skill, producing one document in `docs/`.
- **Skill** — a set of instructions your agent follows to run one step well. You don't need to know their names: say "continue" and your agent picks the right one.
- **Your path** — `docs/PATH.md`: which steps your product runs in each phase, in what order, and why. Your agent writes it as each phase starts.
- **Plan** — `docs/PLAN.md`: a programme your coach or the product audit wrote for you. When it exists, it sets the order.
- **Fast-track** — a step done from what already exists (your code, your site) instead of from scratch.
- **Challenge** — Ship in 7 (live in seven sessions) or Sell in 30 (a first paying customer in thirty), a session-by-session plan with a deadline.

## Your project

- **Repo (repository)** — the folder that holds everything for your product, with version history turned on.
- **Git / version history** — a record of every saved change, so nothing is lost and any change can be undone.
- **Commit** — one saved checkpoint in the version history, with a note on what changed.
- **`docs/` folder** — where every document ProductOS writes for you lives: your offer, design, build plan, growth plan.
- **`productos/` folder** — ProductOS itself. It's kept out of your version history because it's licensed to you, not part of your product.
- **GitHub** — a website that keeps a backup copy of your repo online.

## Building

- **PRD (product requirements document)** — the spec your coding agent builds from: what the product does, screen by screen or feature by feature.
- **Roadmap** — the build plan: the PRD broken into small tasks with checkboxes, in order.
- **MVP (minimum viable product)** — the smallest version that delivers the magic moment to a real customer.
- **Tech stack** — the tools and services the product is built with.
- **Design system** — your colours, fonts, spacing, and styles, written down so every screen matches.
- **Evals** — test cases an AI product must pass, written before the build so "done" has a bar.
- **Code review** — a check of new code for mistakes before it's saved.
- **Security audit** — a check that customers' data and your accounts are safe before going live.
- **Environment variables** — private settings like passwords and keys, kept out of the code so they're never exposed.
- **Deploy** — putting the latest version of the product on the internet (or in a store) where customers can reach it.

## Growing

- **Go-to-market** — your plan for where you'll find customers: the three channels you'll start with.
- **Channel** — a place you reach customers: a marketplace, search, a community, cold email, and so on.
- **Growth experiment** — a small test with a number to hit and a date, so you learn what works.
- **Activation** — a new user reaching the magic moment.
- **Retention** — users coming back.
